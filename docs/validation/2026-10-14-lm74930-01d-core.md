# 2026-10-14 LM74930-Q1 U401 actual controller integration checkpoint

The previously vacant/retired input protection sheet `01D_INPUT_PROTECTION.kicad_sch` now contains the **actual LM74930QRGERQ1 symbol U401** in the shared KiCad V1 schematic hierarchy. This is a physical schematic change, not just a report. Two 1206 100 nF capacitors have been placed as directed by TI, with electrically labeled nets:

- C401: `LM74930_VS` to `GND` (VS bypass).
- C402: `LM74930_CAP` to `LM74930_VS` (charge pump bypass).

The controller's 21 active pins are broken out on individually named global nets `LM74930_...` for further implementation. The three NC signal pins (8/17/21) and exposed floating RTN pad (25) are represented in the pre-existing symbol, which follows TI Table 5-1. The bare RTN pad must **remain electrically isolated and must not be assigned GND or copper fill**; see `docs/LM74930_LIBRARY_AND_CORNERS.md`.

**Critical:** The globally labeled U401 inputs and outputs are currently only pin breakouts. **There is no external power path** until the correct back-to-back FETs, current shunt, surge input protection, VS supply regulator/protection, fault timer, UV/OV divider, ILIM/ISCP network and ACC/EN control are connected. The sole additions here (controller and two decoupling capacitors) cannot be claimed to pass a function test or to supply the LMR36520. The designer must select automotive-rated FETs with verified SOA at cold-crank and load-dump and handle MODE, fault policy, and low-power shutoff.

Pinout and 100-nF capacitor suggestions originate in [TI LM74930-Q1 datasheet, Table 5-1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm74930-q1.pdf), notably CAP pin 23 to VS pin 22, VS pin 22 bypass to GND pin 13. The data sheet specifically warns: do not connect RTN exposed pad to ground. Preliminary footprint `Tripmaster:TI_LM74930_RGE0024T_FLOAT_EP` was created previously. Its physical library checks do not substitute for PCB DRC, manufacturing review or waveform testing.

Remaining engineering checkpoints: (1) high-voltage protection MOSFET orientation and source/drain net connectivity, (2) accurate sense resistor and power dissipation at 6 V, (3) protected VS and cold crank, (4) fail-off ACC boot/MCU HOLD source independent of vehicle-5V USB-only and maximum hold deadline, (5) native ERC/DRC and layout migration. **NO PCB/FAB/VEHICLE APPROVAL.**
